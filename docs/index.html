How the agent qualifies a signal

1WatchTrade news, PR Newswire and 27 news searches for CEO growth plans, plus new SEC Form D capital raises every weekday.
2ReadOpens each article and keeps only ones under 120 days old with a numeric goal.
3AI extractsPulls the goal, the speaker and the exact sentence. Job cuts and one-off openings are dropped.
4Code checksQuote must appear word for word, with the target number in it. Passed deadlines are rejected.
5Company + CEOWeb search for size, ownership, CEO and a financial snapshot, each with a link. Public, subsidiaries, <50 staff, non-US/Canada and revenue outside $10M–$200M are out.
6Score + writeQualified at 60+. Challenge-style copy, a 3-slide PPT and an image card are built from checked facts only.

Good to know

  • Capital raise (Form D) signals come from SEC filings: the company itself filed the revenue band and the amount raised.
  • The agent also runs on its own every weekday at 6:00 AM ET (news) and 6:30 AM ET (SEC Form D). Those runs add signals here but don't show in the run status.
  • Review means a person must check one thing first; the reason is listed on the signal.
  • Some sites block automated reading. Those signals are checked against the news snippet only, so open the article before sending.
  • Each run uses OpenAI credit. A full news scan takes about 3–10 minutes.
